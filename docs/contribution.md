<h1>Contribution</h1>
<h2>Support the Preservation of the Hindu Devotional Collection</h2>
<p>Maintenance | Preservation | Continuity</p>
<p>The Hindu Devotional Collection is maintained as a free, non-commercial devotional reference. Access does not depend upon contribution. Voluntary support helps meet the practical costs of keeping the collection available, secure, backed up and usable over time.</p>
<h2>Why Support Is Needed</h2>
<p>A growing digital collection requires continuing technical care. Contributions are intended only to support reasonable project-related expenditure connected with maintenance, preservation and continuity.</p>

<h2>Our Commitment</h2>
<h3>Free Access Stays Free</h3><p>A contribution is never required to use the collection.</p>
<h3>No Preferential Access</h3><p>Contributors and non-contributors receive the same public access.</p>
<h3>Project-Related Use</h3><p>Support is intended for maintenance, preservation and technical upkeep.</p>
<p>Financial support is appreciated, but it is not the only way to help. Sharing the collection with family, elders, friends and others who may find it useful is equally valuable.</p>
