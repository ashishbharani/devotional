# About Me

<img class="abp-author-portrait" src="../assets/images/ashish-bharani-portrait.png" width="425" height="482" alt="Ashish Bharani" decoding="async">

## Ashish Bharani

**Advocate | Researcher & Compiler**

> A seeker, a learner, and a humble compiler of our rich devotional heritage.

## How It All Began

This project began in the simplest possible way. I was only making a list for my own personal use – something that would keep devotional works organised and make them easier for me to find whenever I needed them. Then I thought of my mother. She is deeply religious, so I felt that a well-organised collection of prayers, mantras, hymns and other devotional works could be useful to her as well.

From there, the thought became a little bigger. If it could help me and help my mother, perhaps it could help the rest of my family too. Then I asked myself – why keep it small? Why not try to make it as complete and useful as possible, so that instead of searching in many different places, a person could find as much as possible in one place? That small list slowly grew into the collection you see today.

## The Influence of My Parents

The inspiration to keep improving this work comes greatly from my parents.

### From my father,

I learned hard work, discipline, perseverance and the desire to achieve the best possible result. Though religiously inclined, he has always remained practical and realistic.

### From my mother,

I learned a more idealistic and deeply devotional way of looking at faith – the belief that prayer, devotion and spirituality can bring comfort, strength and guidance into everyday life.

> Somewhere between my father's realism and my mother's faith, this project found its direction.

## An Advocate by Profession

I am an Advocate by profession, and that naturally influences the way I work on this project.

- Researching and examining information carefully
- Cross-checking and verifying sources
- Organising and classifying material logically
- Distinguishing between different works and identities
- Avoiding conclusions without proper basis
- Remaining open to correction and continuous improvement

These habits help me approach the collection with a structured and research-oriented mindset. I do not present myself as a religious authority, Guru, Acharya or final interpreter of Hindu tradition. I am a compiler, researcher and learner, and it is my sincere effort to bring useful devotional material together in an organised and accessible form.

> To preserve our traditions, understand one another, connect generations, and bring communities and families a little closer.

## A Bridge Between Generations

Many valuable devotional traditions were passed down orally within families. Today, younger generations live in a very different world. I hope this website helps bring these generations closer, so that knowledge, prayers and traditions can continue rather than quietly fade away.

## A Bridge Between Communities

Hindu traditions are wonderfully diverse. People from different regions, languages and communities may follow different customs, deities and devotional practices. I hope this website becomes a meeting point where people can explore and understand each other's traditions with curiosity and respect, recognising both our differences and our deeper shared values.

## Helping People Become Better Partners

When two people from different communities, regions or family traditions choose to build a life together, understanding each other's customs and beliefs becomes very important. I hope this website can help couples and families learn about each other's traditions, reduce misunderstandings and build stronger, more respectful relationships based on knowledge and appreciation.

## Tradition and Technology Can Work Together

I do not believe modern technology has to compete with tradition. Used carefully, it can help preserve it. A scripture, prayer or devotional work that was once difficult to find can today be read, listened to and shared across the world. The website is an effort to make our rich devotional heritage easier to discover, explore and use in today's digital world.

## A Living Project

This is not a finished project. I continue to research, add genuine material, improve classifications, correct errors and include new devotional works as they are discovered. I hope it remains a living collection, shaped by time, learning and the support of many people.

## My Hope

My hope is simple: that this website is useful – to someone searching for a devotional work, to an elderly person looking for something familiar, to a young person discovering a tradition for the first time, to families who wish to preserve their heritage, and to people who want to understand the traditions of the person they love.

If this work can help preserve a little knowledge, create a little curiosity, encourage a little respect and bring a few people closer together, then every hour spent building it will have been worthwhile.

> What began as something for myself became something for my mother; what began for my mother became something for my family; and what began for my family, I now offer to everyone.

With gratitude and Pranam,

**Ashish Bharani**

Advocate | Researcher & Compiler
