# About Me

<img class="abp-author-portrait" src="../assets/images/ashish-bharani-portrait.png" width="425" height="482" alt="Ashish Bharani" decoding="async">

## Ashish Bharani

**Advocate | Researcher & Compiler**

I am an Advocate by profession, but this website grew from a part of my life that exists beyond my professional work.

It began very simply. I wanted to organise devotional works for my own use so that prayers, mantras, hymns, bhajans and other material could be found without searching through countless different places. Then I began thinking about my mother and how useful a properly organised collection could be for her. From there, the idea gradually expanded to my family, to older generations, to younger people, and eventually to anyone who may wish to understand or reconnect with our religious and cultural traditions.

My intention has never been merely to create a large catalogue. I wanted to create something that people could actually use — something that helps an elderly person find a familiar prayer, helps a younger person discover tradition, and helps families pass meaningful knowledge from one generation to the next.

I also hope this platform becomes a bridge between communities. Even within Hindu traditions, people from different regions, languages and family backgrounds often follow different customs and devotional practices. By understanding each other’s traditions better, people can grow in respect, compassion and closeness.

I especially hope it helps couples and families from different communities understand one another more deeply and become better partners in life. If this work helps preserve tradition, reconnect people with their roots, and bring communities and families a little closer, then every hour spent building it will have been worthwhile.

> To preserve our traditions, understand one another, connect generations, and bring communities and families a little closer.

With gratitude and Pranam,

**Ashish Bharani**
