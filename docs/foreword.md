# Foreword

**Dedicated to my parents**

## A small beginning

This collection began as a small personal devotional list — prayers, mantras, hymns and bhajans brought together for my own use. While organising it, I began thinking of my mother: her faith, the prayers familiar to her, and how useful a properly arranged collection could be. From that thought, the work expanded toward my family, and then beyond it. Why should it stop with us?

## Why the collection grew

An elder may be looking for a prayer remembered from childhood. A parent may wish to share a devotional work with a child. A younger person may be searching for a connection with tradition. Material is scattered across languages, books, recordings and regions; bringing it together can make that inheritance easier to discover and use.

The aim is to preserve and organise, not simply to accumulate. The collection makes space for prayers, hymns, devotional music, saints, festivals and the many regional and family traditions that form our shared heritage. It seeks to connect people with that inheritance and with one another.

## From a collection and book to a website

The website is another chapter of the same journey. It makes the collection searchable and allows readers to explore devotional works, read, listen and share, alongside scriptures and supporting reference tools. The purpose has not changed with the medium: faith, memory, family and the connection between generations remain at its heart.

A familiar prayer may offer comfort. An old bhajan may bring back a memory. A younger reader may discover something a parent or grandparent knew well. The value of the work lies in such moments, not merely in the number of entries.

## Between realism and faith

My father’s example speaks of hard work, discipline, perseverance, responsibility and practical effort. My mother’s faith speaks of strength, comfort, hope and peace. These influences belong together: one encourages the work to continue, while the other reminds me why it matters.

## A living collection

This heritage has travelled for centuries from grandparents to parents and children, and from teachers to disciples. Technology can help it reach more homes, but the collection remains a living work. It cannot claim to be perfect or complete. Errors, omissions, broken links and incomplete information may remain; responsible corrections and improvements are welcome.

The intention is to document traditions respectfully and keep the collection useful as it grows. Preservation does not end with gathering material: it also requires care, understanding and passing it on.

## Passing our heritage forward

What began with a personal list and thoughts of my mother has grown from family toward people and homes I may never know. May it help someone remember an old prayer, rediscover a forgotten work, find comfort, or awaken curiosity and devotion. May a child carry something meaningful further into the next generation.

To my parents, this work carries gratitude for the values, discipline and faith that shaped it. The book was a beginning; the website continues that journey. If it helps our heritage remain living and shared, that is enough.

Please use, explore, share, help improve and pass this collection forward.

With Pranam,

**Ashish Bharani — Collector & Compiler**
